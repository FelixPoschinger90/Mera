MERA · NORTHERN OUTPOST · RESEARCH FIELD SIMULATION

Enter the reserve.

You are an environmental field intern assigned to a remote reserve outpost beyond normal signal coverage. Last night, the reserve was hit by a devastating storm. Members of your team are injured and missing. Your only guide is MERA, the reserve’s AI navigation system. You must reach the Northern Outpost and restore its emergency uplink so that emergency services can be contacted.

YOUR GUIDEMERA will provide spoken navigation over the field link. Her terrain data is incomplete, so some route decisions will be yours.
CONTROLSWASD move · Shift run · Space jump · mouse drag camera

Research information. Gameplay behaviour, route choices, timings, movement, stimulus exposure and the written responses are recorded under a randomly generated session ID.
